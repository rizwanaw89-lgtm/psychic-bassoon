French Fries Recipe

Ingredients

  • 3-4 large potatoes
  • Oil (vegetable or sunflower oil)
  • Salt (to taste)

Instructions (Crispy Classic Fries)

  1. Prepare the potatoes
    • Wash and peel (optional).
    • Cut into long thin strips.
  2. Soak the potatoes
    • Put the cut potatoes in cold water for 30 minutes.
    • This removes extra starch and makes fries crispy.
  3. Dry well
    • Drain and pat dry with a clean towel.
  4. First fry (soft inside)
    • Heat oil to medium heat.
    • Fry potatoes for 3-4 minutes (do not brown).
    • Remove and rest for 5 minutes.
  5. Second fry (crispy outside)
    • Heat oil to high heat.
    • Fry again for 2-3 minutes until golden brown.
  6. Season & serve
    • Remove fries, drain excess oil.
    • Sprinkle with salt and serve hot

Easy Alternatives

  • Air fryer fries: Toss with a little oil, cook at 180°C / 360°F for 15-18 min
  • Oven fries: Bake at 220°C / 425°F for 30-35 min, flipping halfway
